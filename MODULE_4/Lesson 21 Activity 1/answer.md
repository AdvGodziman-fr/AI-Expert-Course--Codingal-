### How to Pick Your Next Adventure

1. **Define Your Interest** – Nature, culture, food, adventure, history, or relaxation?  
2. **Consider the Season** – Some places (e.g., Patagonia in summer) are only beautiful in certain months.  
3. **Budget & Time** – Major hubs (e.g., Tokyo, New York) cost more but offer easy onward connections.  
4. **Travel Restrictions** – Check current visa or health‑related requirements for each country.
